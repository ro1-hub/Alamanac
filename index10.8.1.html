The entrepreneur's compass

Strategy, distilled

The full plan behind the daily push above — vision, model, offer, and the next moves.

Vision

Build a healing community focused on natural healing, spinal therapies, gardening, and mindfulness — starting in Croatia. 6-12 months: transition into paid health coaching. 3-5 years: a full enterprise spanning food, coaching, and a connected resource network.

The model

Start: Personalized Health Coaching — fastest to a first client, needs only a room. Grow into: the Healing Community Hub — workshops, membership, digital resources. Later upsell: Natural Healing Retreats, once there's a client base to fill one.

Other reference ideas kept on file: AI wellness resources, community gardening, healing content/YouTube, meditation apps, spinal-therapy workshops, a membership platform, healing kits, self-defense classes, a certification track.

Ideal client

Ana or Marko, 30-45, burned out by a high-stress job, already circling yoga studios, farmers' markets, and wellness groups. Wants calm, mobility, and clarity — worried about cost, time, and being judged.

Positioning & offer

"Natural Healing, Tailored to Your Body, Mind, and Heart."

Session 1 — Discovery: assessment, foundational mindfulness, simple spinal stretches → a tailored wellness plan.
Session 2 — Restoration: gardening/movement, spinal alignment → a Daily Healing Checklist.
Session 3 — Realignment: emotional release, progress review → a 30-Day Healing Roadmap.

€150 for the 3-session package, 60-90 min, in-person or virtual · upsells: monthly membership, 1:1 coaching, topic workshops.

BATON plan

Audience: free local workshops, simple Instagram + short videos.
Tribe: an email list built on a free "5 Steps" download.
Offer: the Holistic Reset package, priced to attract the first tribe.
Network: cross-promote with yoga/massage/chiropractic practitioners.

First 90 days

Weeks 1-4: 2-3 free workshops, 50-100 flyers, awareness only. Weeks 5-8: open paid coaching at an intro rate, run one small paid class. Weeks 9-12: collect testimonials, refine pricing, start saving toward a website and email list.
